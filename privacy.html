How we handle visitor data

Privacy

This page describes how The Corridor handles visitor data. The publication's principle is to collect as little personal data as possible.

What we collect

The site uses minimal analytics to understand which issues are being read and from where. The data is aggregated and anonymised. Individual readers are not identified.

Subscription to the email newsletter is handled by Substack and governed by Substack's own privacy policy. The Corridor receives only the email addresses of subscribers.

Cookies

The site uses essential cookies only. See the Cookies page for detail on each cookie used.

Your rights

Subscribers may unsubscribe at any time using the link at the foot of every newsletter. To request deletion of any personal data the publication holds, write to privacy@thecorridorafrica.com.
