Issue 024 · 3 August 2026 · Continental

Four African countries have ratified the continent's free-movement protocol in seven years. The trade deal moves goods faster than the continent moves people.

Africa's leaders adopted a free-movement protocol in January 2018 and a continental free-trade area two months later, meant to move goods and people together. Seven years on, the trade area is operational while the mobility protocol has four ratifications, the most recent in July 2019. Into that vacuum, individual states moved alone with opposite results: Rwanda opened to every African visa-free and scored a perfect 1.000 on the AfDB index; Kenya announced the same opening, executed it as a fee-bearing digital pre-authorisation, and fell seventeen places. On the one lever a state controls completely, the announcement is not the policy.
