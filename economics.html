Issue 030 · 14 September 2026 · Continental

African pension funds are buying the continent's hotels. Five foreign brands keep 80 percent of the control.

African Capital, Foreign Control. Africa's hotel pipeline is a record 123,846 rooms, and five foreign brands hold roughly 80 percent of it. Increasingly the money is African — pension funds in South Africa, Zambia and Tanzania taking the freehold. But owning the building is not controlling it: under the management contract the brand takes a fee on revenue, a fee on profit, mandatory brand-standard spend and a booking charge, across a 20-to-30-year term the owner cannot cheaply escape. The continent is winning the ownership and losing the control.
