Errors corrected and policy

Corrections

The Corridor publishes corrections to issued analysis when errors are identified. This page lists all corrections issued since the publication launched.

Submitting a correction

Readers who identify factual errors are invited to submit them to corrections@thecorridorafrica.com. Submissions should identify the issue, the specific claim, the error and the corrected information with sources.

How corrections are handled

The Corridor reviews every submitted correction. Where the correction is verified, it is published on this page and noted at the top of the affected issue. Substantive corrections are also disclosed in the next published Monday issue.
