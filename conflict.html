Issue 017 · 15 June 2026 · West Africa

The Gambia grew 46 percent in 2025. The structural cause is the Sahel coups two borders to the east.

Five coups, one confederation, one ECOWAS exit, one biometric passport and a set of mutual travel bans have closed roughly 2.78 million square kilometres of the Sahel to global leisure tourism. The flow did not disappear. It moved to the coast. The Gambia is the most measurable beneficiary.
